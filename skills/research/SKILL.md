# Research Skill

## When to use this skill
Investigating a specific question, comparing approaches, or needing to
justify a decision with evidence.

## Instructions
1. Start from the specific question being asked; avoid producing a
   generic survey of the topic.
2. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs, changelogs) over
   secondhand summaries or blog recaps.
3. Track a citation for every non-obvious claim.
4. When sources disagree, state the disagreement explicitly rather than
   silently picking one side.
